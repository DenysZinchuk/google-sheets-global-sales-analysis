# Global Sales Analysis | Google Sheets

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data for a company operating in global markets through online and offline sales channels.

The analysis was performed in Google Sheets and covers product categories, geographical performance, sales channels, shipping time, sales trends, weekdays, and ABC analysis.

## Dataset

The dataset consists of three tables:

- `events.csv` — sales transactions
- `products.csv` — product categories and product codes
- `countries.csv` — countries, regions, subregions and country codes

## Tools

- Google Sheets
- Pivot Tables
- Google Sheets formulas
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization
- ABC Analysis
- Pareto Analysis

## Analysis

The project includes:

- Data quality and integrity checks
- Revenue, cost and profit calculations
- KPI analysis
- Product category analysis
- Country, region and subregion analysis
- Online vs offline sales analysis
- Shipping time analysis
- Yearly and monthly sales trends
- Weekday sales analysis
- ABC analysis
- Interactive dashboard

## Key Insights

- Office Supplies generated the highest revenue and was classified as an A-category product group based on sales, revenue and profit.
- Cosmetics was the most profitable product category.
- Offline sales generated higher profit than online sales.
- Western and Eastern Europe were among the strongest regions by profit.
- Profit reached its peak in 2012 and generally declined afterwards.
- Monday and Friday were among the most profitable days.
- Shipping time did not show a clear linear relationship with profit.

## Files

- `global_sales_analysis.xlsx` — complete analysis and dashboard
- `sales_analysis_report.pdf` — business conclusions and recommendations
